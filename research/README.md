# Research foundation

This directory documents an unvalidated construct and a proposed research program. No human-subject study has been conducted or authorized by creating these files.

- [Construct definition](construct-definition.md): the three profiles and the narrow unit of interpretation.
- [Literature review](literature-review.md): conceptual synthesis with linked sources.
- [Competing frameworks](competing-frameworks.md): rival explanations and discriminating observations.
- [Validity threats](validity-threats.md): what each inference would require.
- [Research questions](research-questions.md) and [roadmap](research-roadmap.md).
- [Annotated bibliography](annotated-bibliography.md), [BibTeX](references.bib), [source register](sources.json), and [verification audit](source-verification.md).
- [Seven planned studies](studies/README.md): study-specific hypotheses, outcomes, sampling, analysis, alternatives, missingness, preregistration and limitations.
- [Institutional review protocol](ethics/irb-protocol.md), [draft consent](ethics/consent-v0.1.md), [privacy/security plan](ethics/privacy-security-plan.md), [risk/benefit assessment](ethics/risk-benefit.md), and [recruitment draft](ethics/recruitment.md).

The JSON discovery records are bibliographic metadata, not research results. Synthetic engineering fixtures elsewhere in the repository must never be presented as observations of participants. The study plans require a responsible investigator, institutional determination, locked methods and actual registration before collection. Local play does not imply research enrollment.
