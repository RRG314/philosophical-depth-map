# Annotated bibliography

Discovery foundation, 10 October 2026. These are starting sources, not a systematic review or a claim that every work was fully read. Relevance notes are design interpretations.

## Dewey1910

Dewey, John (1910). *How We Think*. D. C. Heath. [Source](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37423).

Inquiry connects uncertainty to testing reasons; activity alone is not reflection.

Verification: Primary text excerpts and metadata inspected.

## Arendt1971

Arendt, Hannah (1971). *Thinking and Moral Considerations: A Lecture*. Social Research 38(3), 417–446. [Source](https://www.jstor.org/stable/i40043605).

A philosophical question about thinking and moral judgment; not evidence that philosophy guarantees moral conduct.

Verification: Journal issue listing and source PDF metadata inspected.

## Horkheimer1947

Horkheimer, Max (1947). *Eclipse of Reason*. Oxford University Press. [Source](https://search.worldcat.org/title/531879).

A critique of reason restricted to means; a conceptual antecedent rather than a psychological diagnosis.

Verification: Library metadata and primary excerpt located; book not fully read.

## Habermas1981

Habermas, Jürgen (1981). *Theorie des kommunikativen Handelns*. Suhrkamp. [Source](https://www.suhrkamp.de/rights/book/juergen-habermas-the-theory-of-communicative-action-fr-9783518287750).

Communicative justification makes social context relevant to the design.

Verification: Publisher description and original publication metadata inspected.

## Rawls1971

Rawls, John (1971). *A Theory of Justice*. Harvard University Press. [Source](https://doi.org/10.4159/9780674042605).

Considered judgments and principles motivate testing reflective integration without scoring ideological agreement.

Verification: Publisher and Crossref resolver metadata; full text not read.

## KingKitchener1994

King, Patricia M. and Kitchener, Karen Strohm (1994). *Developing Reflective Judgment*. Jossey-Bass. [Source](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED368925).

Ill-structured problems and justification; established research program distinct from the proposed construct.

Verification: Metadata and abstract/description inspected; not full text.

## Facione1990

Facione, Peter A. (1990). *Critical Thinking: A Statement of Expert Consensus for Purposes of Educational Assessment and Instruction*. ERIC ED315423. [Source](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED315423).

Expert consensus offers candidate skill vocabulary, not validation of this rubric.

Verification: Metadata and abstract/description inspected; not full text.

## HoferPintrich1997

Hofer, Barbara K. and Pintrich, Paul R. (1997). *The Development of Epistemological Theories: Beliefs About Knowledge and Knowing and Their Relation to Learning*. Review of Educational Research 67(1), 88–140. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543067001088).

Competing definitions of epistemic cognition caution against treating the new label as an independent construct.

Verification: Metadata and abstract/description inspected; not full text.

## Leary2017

Leary, Mark R. and Diebels, Kate J. and Davisson, Erin K. and Jongman-Sereno, Katrina P. and Isherwood, Jennifer C. and Raimi, Kaitlin T. and Deffler, Samantha A. and Hoyle, Rick H. (2017). *Cognitive and Interpersonal Features of Intellectual Humility*. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin 43(6), 793–813. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167217697695).

Fallibility-related dispositions are relevant external measures, not substitutes for scored performance.

Verification: Metadata and abstract/description inspected; not full text.

## Kohlberg1969

Kohlberg, Lawrence (1969). *Stage and Sequence: The Cognitive-developmental Approach to Socialization*. In D. A. Goslin, Handbook of Socialization Theory and Research, 347–480. Rand McNally. [Source](https://books.google.com/books?id=2tHCHAAACAAJ).

A developmental comparison; the platform does not assume universal moral stages.

Verification: Title/author/year verified in book record; chapter pagination corroborated in bibliographies, primary chapter not inspected.

## Raz2005

Raz, Joseph (2005). *Instrumental Rationality: A Reprise*. Journal of Ethics and Social Philosophy 1(1), 1–20. [Source](https://doi.org/10.26556/jesp.v1i1.14).

Distinguishing instrumental reasons from rationality clarifies the proposed means–ends contrast.

Verification: PhilPapers metadata and abstract inspected.

## BarnettCeci2002

Barnett, Susan M. and Ceci, Stephen J. (2002). *When and Where Do We Apply What We Learn? A Taxonomy for Far Transfer*. Psychological Bulletin 128(4), 612–637. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.128.4.612).

Transfer needs specified distances and contexts; repeated questions do not demonstrate generalization.

Verification: Primary PDF abstract and introduction excerpt inspected.

## KooLi2016

Koo, Terry K. and Li, Mae Y. (2016). *A Guideline of Selecting and Reporting Intraclass Correlation Coefficients for Reliability Research*. Journal of Chiropractic Medicine 15(2), 155–163. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcm.2016.02.012).

Report the chosen ICC model, unit and agreement definition; ordinal ratings also need ordinal agreement checks. Erratum exists: 10.1016/j.jcm.2017.10.001.

Verification: Metadata and abstract/description inspected; not full text.

## Samejima1969

Samejima, Fumiko (1969). *Estimation of Latent Ability Using a Response Pattern of Graded Scores*. Psychometrika 34 1-97. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/bf03372160).

Graded-response IRT is a possible future ordinal model requiring calibration and fit checks.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; full text not inspected.

## Kuhn1991

Kuhn, Deanna (1991). *The Skills of Argument*. Cambridge University Press. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1017/cbo9780511571350).

Arguments, alternatives and rebuttals inform the episode structure; expertise remains a rival explanation.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; abstract/description inspected.

## ShuteVentura2013

Shute, Valerie and Ventura, Matthew (2013). *Stealth Assessment*. The MIT Press. [Source](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/9589.001.0001).

Game-based evidence needs an explicit inference model; rewards are not assessment validity.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; abstract/description inspected.

## Horn1965

Horn, John L. (1965). *A Rationale and Test for the Number of Factors in Factor Analysis*. Psychometrika 30 179-185. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/bf02289447).

Parallel analysis can inform dimensionality; it cannot replace substantive interpretation.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; full text not inspected.

## Yang2024

Yang, Kaixun and Raković, Mladen and Li, Yuyang and Guan, Quanlong and Gašević, Dragan and Chen, Guangliang (2024). *Unveiling the Tapestry of Automated Essay Scoring: A Comprehensive Investigation of Accuracy, Fairness, and Generalizability*. Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence 38 22466-22474. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i20.30254).

AES fairness and generalization require evaluation separate from predictive accuracy.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; abstract/description inspected.

## Kane2013

Kane, Michael T. (2013). *Validating the Interpretations and Uses of Test Scores*. Journal of Educational Measurement 50 1-73. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.12000).

Validation concerns particular interpretations and uses, not a universal validated-test badge.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; abstract/description inspected.

## Flavell1979

Flavell, John H. (1979). *Metacognition and cognitive monitoring: A new area of cognitive–developmental inquiry.*. American Psychologist 34 906-911. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066x.34.10.906).

Monitoring thinking differs from knowing philosophical vocabulary.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; full text not inspected.

## Abrami2015

Abrami, Philip C. and Bernard, Robert M. and Borokhovski, Eugene and Waddington, David I. and Wade, C. Anne and Persson, Tonje (2015). *Strategies for Teaching Students to Think Critically*. Review of Educational Research 85 275-314. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654314551063).

Instructional evidence motivates a controlled study, not a claim that this game works.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; abstract/description inspected.

## Muis2021

Muis, Krista R. and Chevrier, Marianne and Denton, Courtney A. and Losenno, Kelsey M. (2021). *Epistemic Emotions and Epistemic Cognition Predict Critical Thinking About Socio-Scientific Issues*. Frontiers in Education 6. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2021.669908).

OpenAlex discovery expands consideration of emotion and epistemic cognition as competing explanations.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; full text not inspected.

## Lee2024

Lee, Jinsook and Hicke, Yann and Yu, Renzhe and Brooks, Christopher and Kizilcec, René F. (2024). *The life cycle of large language models in education: A framework for understanding sources of bias*. British Journal of Educational Technology 55 1982-2002. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1111/bjet.13505).

Bias can enter throughout an educational LLM lifecycle; a generic safety prompt is insufficient.

Verification: Crossref exact DOI metadata verified; abstract/description inspected.

## SEPReflectiveEquilibrium

Knight, Carl (2023). *Reflective Equilibrium*. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. [Source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/reflective-equilibrium/).

Reference orientation to wide and narrow equilibrium; do not infer truth from coherence.

Verification: Entry passages and author metadata inspected.

## SEPValuePluralism

Mason, Elinor (2023). *Value Pluralism*. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy; accessed 10 October 2026. [Source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/value-pluralism/).

Irreducible values warn against grading convergence on one moral conclusion.

Verification: Entry excerpts and author metadata inspected; 2023 copyright revision.

## IEPSocrates

Ambury, James M. (2026). *Socrates*. Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy; accessed 10 October 2026. [Source](https://iep.utm.edu/socrates/).

Question-and-answer motivates invitations to inquiry; historical Socrates must not be equated with a dialogue bot.

Verification: Entry passages and author information inspected; year is access year, not original publication.
