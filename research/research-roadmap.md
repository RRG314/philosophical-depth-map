# Research roadmap

Research enrollment is disabled. No IRB approval or exemption, participants, empirical results, calibrated difficulties, population norms, or validated scores are claimed.

| Gate | Work | Acceptance evidence | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Construct and item review | Documented disagreement; revised opportunities; accessibility interviews | Ethics determination before collecting human-subject data |
| B | Rater training and reliability | Prespecified agreement estimates and intervals by dimension | Stable rubric and blinded independent ratings |
| C | Structure and validity | Held-out model checks, competing models, external measures, DIF | Adequate category coverage and simulation-informed sample |
| D | Means–ends dissociation | Repeated within-person evidence with competing explanations | Reliable independent task families |
| E | Guided exploration trial | Registered contrast, pre/post and novel transfer outcomes | Safe deployed intervention, allocation, consent, power plan |
| F | AI agreement and bias | Model/version-specific evidence; style, position and tradition audits | Frozen evaluator and human reference process |
| G | Longitudinal transfer | Delayed outcomes and attrition sensitivity | Follow-up consent and sustainable retention |

Implementation milestones are not scientific gates. Version 1 may be a usable static game while the instrument remains unvalidated. Version 2 adds authorized infrastructure and human-review workflows; its existence does not permit enrollment automatically. Version 3 requires actual calibration and invariance evidence before latent estimates. Version 4 adds approved collaborations and registered multi-site replication. Version 5 may add moderated social features only after moderation and privacy review.

Planning costs are not quotes: expert review and translation require specialist labor; independent double rating is likely a larger cost than static hosting; AI costs depend on locked model, tokens and dose. Each study needs a budget and responsible institution before activation. Establish a minimum effect or precision target with stakeholders before sample-size simulation. Do not choose a sample merely because an IRT package accepts it.

Before release, broaden the current predominantly Anglophone discovery review through specialists in African, Indigenous, Islamic, South Asian and East Asian philosophy. Their participation must influence assumptions and scoring, not merely add decorative names. The current sources establish starting points, not cultural coverage.
