# Construct definition

Version 0.1 · 10 October 2026 · Proposed, unvalidated construct

“Philosophical depth” is a working name for the quality of examination a person demonstrates, on a particular occasion, of a claim's assumptions, reasons, conceptual distinctions, alternatives, implications, and relationship to other commitments. It is not a diagnosis, a measure of personal worth, or a discovered general faculty. “Philosophical deficit” remains a motivating hypothesis: capable practical performance can coexist with limited demonstrated examination of the ends or premises of that performance. The platform should describe observed evidence, not label people deficient.

The unit of inference is an answer-in-context, including its prompt and permitted follow-ups. A short answer may demonstrate a precise distinction. An eloquent answer may contain no justification. A participant may correctly object that a question presupposes the conclusion. Silence can reflect privacy, fatigue, accessibility, language, lack of opportunity, or refusal to accept a framing; it is not evidence of inability. Rejecting an objection after answering it can be as reflective as revising a belief.

## Three profiles

1. **Knowledge:** concept recognition, argument comprehension, and application. Recognition is not equivalent to productive reasoning. Conversely, limited vocabulary can hide sound reasoning.
2. **Reasoning:** justification, assumption awareness, counterargument engagement, consistency, conceptual precision, and reflective integration. Implication tracing belongs within justification and integration. Scores require observable evidence and an actual opportunity to show the skill.
3. **Exploration:** questions visited, concepts encountered, connections explored, revisits, and revisions. These are activities. Neither their sum nor an ocean milestone estimates proficiency.

## Defeasible operational proposal

Let the response episode include position, reason, fixed objection, and reply. Independent raters assign 0–4 for each applicable dimension, with quoted evidence and explanation. An unobserved dimension is NA. The six-dimensional profile is retained rather than collapsed into a universal depth number. Domain summaries are descriptive summaries of sampled performances and must show coverage. Repeated ratings of one response are not independent demonstrations.

A provisional dissociation is supported only if the same participant repeatedly performs well on separately justified instrumental tasks while demonstrating less foundational examination across accessible, well-understood opportunities. This requires reliable measures on both sides and a defensible comparison; subtracting arbitrary raw totals does not establish a deficit. Demonstrating the dissociation would not establish its prevalence, cause, harm, or remediation.

## What would challenge the construct

If language proficiency, task familiarity, or motivation explains the putative distinction, rename or abandon it. If dimensions do not cohere, report narrower skills. If ratings reward particular conclusions despite matched reasoning, revise the rubric and invalidate affected comparisons. If improvement disappears on unfamiliar tasks, restrict claims to coached performance. If experts cannot agree on what counts as evidence without imposing a philosophical tradition, publish that disagreement rather than manufacturing consensus.

The proposed distinction is separable from the thesis that reflection is always good. Deliberation can rationalize prejudice, consume scarce time, or displace warranted trust. The game invites inquiry; it cannot determine how much reflection every life requires.
