# Human-subjects protocol packet

Draft 0.1 · 10 October 2026 · Prepared for institutional review; NOT submitted or approved

## Administrative gate

No research recruitment or remote submission is authorized. A responsible principal investigator, sponsoring institution, jurisdiction-specific review determination, data controller, contact address, hosting region, processors, compensation budget, and approved retention schedule must be supplied before activation. These are administrative requirements, not fictitious completed fields. The publicly usable local game remains available without research consent.

## Purpose and procedures

Evaluate whether a proposed profile of philosophical reasoning can be interpreted fairly, reliably and usefully. Studies A–G in ../studies/ specify distinct questions and designs. Adult volunteers would encounter philosophical prompts, respond, consider objections and optionally revise. Depending on consent and study, independent human raters and a named AI provider may evaluate deidentified responses; some studies randomize guidance, others request delayed follow-up. No moral, intelligence or clinical diagnosis is made.

Plan approximately 30–60 minutes for an initial research session, with shorter separately scheduled follow-ups if applicable; pilot burden before finalizing consent. These are proposed ranges, not measured completion times. Participation is voluntary. Research refusal cannot reduce game access, and skipping questions must remain possible.

## Population and recruitment

Initial research is adults only. Recruit beyond philosophy students where feasible to avoid restricting range. Do not enroll minors without a separate approved protocol. Avoid instructor–student or employer–employee coercion. If compensation is later offered, specify prorating and withdrawal payment before review. Collect only self-reported eligibility necessary for enrollment; do not infer demographics, religion or political identity from text.

## Risks and mitigation

Prompts may cause discomfort, expose personal commitments or generate self-judgment. Provide content descriptions, skip/stop controls, and descriptive rather than diagnostic feedback. Privacy risks include sensitive free text, linkage to contact details, provider processing and reidentification from quotations. Ask participants not to name others, separate contact information, restrict raw access, screen intended quotations, and obtain distinct quotation permission. The system is not counseling and should not invite crisis narratives.

AI may produce inaccurate or upsetting guidance. Identify AI output, permit immediate exit, preserve complaints, and use a human incident-response owner. Stop the study following a material confidentiality incident or unexpected serious harm pending institutional review. Software developers cannot independently determine legal exemption.

## Benefits and alternatives

No personal educational benefit is guaranteed. Possible benefits are enjoyable reflection and contribution to research. The alternative is the same local game without enrollment, or no participation. No direct benefit justifies hidden collection.

## Consent and withdrawal

Use the versioned draft in consent-v0.1.md after institution-specific completion. Obtain consent before recording research events. Separate research participation, external AI processing, optional demographics, follow-up contact and quotation permission. Record consent version and timestamp. Withdrawal stops new collection and invokes the approved deletion workflow; limits for already aggregated results must be explained accurately.

## Data and oversight

See privacy-security-plan.md and risk-benefit.md. Access is role-based; human rating exports exclude contact and assignment where blinding requires it. Every export must be logged. Researchers do not receive authentication secrets. No public raw dataset or verbatim quotation without a specific approved release and reidentification review. An institutional reviewer must approve or determine the applicable requirements before enrollment is enabled.
