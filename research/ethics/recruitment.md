# Recruitment draft — not for circulation before authorization

Would you like to help study how people explore philosophical questions? We are preparing a study of explanations, objections and feedback in a philosophy game. The study would involve adults responding to questions and, in some conditions, trying different forms of guidance. Participation would be optional and would not assess your intelligence or moral worth. You could skip questions or stop, and the game would remain available without participation.

Before this text is used, add the approved investigator and institution, verified contact and eligibility route, exact session duration, compensation if any, study authorization information where appropriate, privacy summary and accessible consent link. Do not advertise the study as approved or open until those requirements are actually met.
