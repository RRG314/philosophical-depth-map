# Draft consent information

Version 0.1 · Research enrollment currently closed

We are studying how people explain philosophical ideas and how feedback is evaluated. If this study receives the necessary authorization and you choose to join, we would ask you to respond to questions, consider objections and sometimes return for follow-up. The exact study, duration, investigator and contact details must be shown before you are asked to consent.

You may skip a question, stop or withdraw. You can use the game without joining research. There is no proven educational benefit and your answers do not diagnose intelligence, morality or a condition. Questions and feedback may be uncomfortable. Avoid names, contact information and private details about yourself or other people in answers.

Research records may include your responses and revisions, question and rubric versions, assigned study condition, ratings and explanations, and limited technical events such as model version, latency and errors. If external AI is used, its named provider, processing location where known, retention terms and data sent must be disclosed separately before use. Refusing external AI processing must not block the ordinary local game.

Your records would be associated with a pseudonymous identifier. Pseudonymous does not mean anonymous: free text can identify people. A separately stored withdrawal code would allow a deletion request through a real investigator contact. The approved retention period, backup deletion interval and limits after aggregation must be filled in before enrollment. No contact channel or deletion capability is claimed to exist merely because this draft describes it.

Required future choices, none preselected: adult eligibility; voluntary participation in the named study under this consent version. Separate optional choices: external AI processing; follow-up contact; demographic questions; use of screened anonymous quotations. No blanket agreement to future studies. Downloadable consent and a working contact/withdrawal route are activation requirements.
