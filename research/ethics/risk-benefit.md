# Risk and benefit assessment

| Risk | Likelihood/severity assessment needed | Mitigation | Residual concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exposure of sensitive beliefs | Depends on prompts and disclosure | Minimize text, skip option, private storage | Free text can remain identifying |
| Stigma from scores | Plausible even in a game | No deficit labels or population ranking | Participants may still compare themselves |
| Distressing topic or AI reply | Requires pilot and incident review | Clear exits, restrained prompts, report channel | Unanticipated personal relevance |
| Ideological or linguistic bias | Not yet measured | Blinded audit, matched variants, appeal | Training data and raters can share bias |
| Reidentification in publication | Depends on detail and audience | No raw public release; quote consent and review | Unique wording can be searchable |
| Coercive recruitment | Context dependent | Independent recruitment; equal game access | Teacher/employer power persists |

No direct benefit is guaranteed. Optional reflection and research contribution do not establish that risks are minimal under any particular regulatory category. The responsible institution must make that determination. Review risks again after pilot changes, new model providers, additional languages, social features, or proposed child participation.
