# Philosophical procedures as experimental interventions

This review supports a software operationalization, not a claim that philosophy has been reduced to four algorithms. The primary research question is whether a specified dialogue procedure changes task-relative reasoning performance compared with an active neutral dialogue. Historical fidelity and educational effectiveness are different questions. Bibliographic metadata, access levels and annotations are recorded in [the bibliography](ANNOTATED_BIBLIOGRAPHY.md); all empirical effects remain unmeasured.

## Descartes: suspension, dependence and reconstruction

In the *Discourse*, Descartes presents disciplined inquiry through caution, division, ordered thinking and review. A procedure that merely multiplies objections would miss the constructive orientation of that method. The Lab consequently asks for an argument reconstruction before and after skeptical examination. This is an interpretive motivation, not a deduction that a graph represents Cartesian thought. [Descartes1637](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/59)

The *Meditations* places doubt within a search for secure foundations. Its first-person exercise, cogito and theological reconstruction cannot be reproduced by checking a list of propositions. The engine takes the narrower operational step of withholding support from a challenged dependency chain. It does not infer falsity from dubitability or declare an unchallenged proposition indubitable. The publisher edition and contemporary methodological discussion support keeping these distinctions explicit. [Descartes1641](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/descartes-meditations-on-first-philosophy/23589F724981000827E1B2674169C934), [SEPDescartesMethod](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/descartes-method/)

Computationally, accepted commitments and their consequences are distinct from grounded support. A finite least-fixed-point calculation prevents a cycle from grounding itself. This is a design choice inspired by dependency-sensitive inquiry, not an implementation of Cartesian intuition or a proof of foundationalism.

## Socratic elenchus: commitments rather than question counts

The definitional exchanges of *Euthyphro* motivate asking what a proposed account means and testing it against acknowledged cases. *Gorgias* makes the substantive ethical and interpersonal setting difficult to ignore. The dialogues are literary and philosophical works, not transcripts of a standardized intervention. [PlatoEuthyphro](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1642), [PlatoGorgias](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1672)

Vlastos’s reconstruction and the disagreements collected by Scott caution against treating “the Socratic method” as settled. A contradictory set of commitments does not, by itself, establish which commitment is false. The Lab therefore records explicit incompatible pairs, selects an authored challenge, invites a distinction or revision, and permits unresolved disagreement. It does not award points for changing one’s mind or asking more questions. [Vlastos1994](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/socratic-studies/socratic-elenchus-method-is-all/E4678880B70286DEB5CDAFE5992638C6), [Scott2002](https://www.psupress.org/books/titles/0-271-02173-X.html)

The deterministic engine recognizes only declared incompatibilities. A prose definition that contradicts another prose statement will not be detected unless that relation is encoded. This limitation is essential to the interpretation of both failures and successes.

## Dewey: a problematic situation and inquiry through consequences

*How We Think* connects reflective thought with an unsettled situation, possible explanations, reasoning and examination. That sequence motivates the problem–hypothesis–consequence–evidence structure used here. A displayed consequence is a prediction, not an observation; a recorded outcome is a participant’s declared input, not evidence collected by the software. [Dewey1910](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37423)

*Logic: The Theory of Inquiry* and contemporary Dewey scholarship place inquiry within the transformation of situations. Deweyan inquiry should not be equated with accepting whatever happens to be convenient. The protocol asks what would count as evidence and which value judgments remain. A practical result can inform a normative question without settling it by itself. The 1938 work is represented here through verified edition information and contemporary interpretation; a full critical reading of its edition is not claimed. [Dewey1938](https://www.siupress.com/9780809312689/the-later-works-of-john-dewey-volume-12-1925-1953/), [SEPDewey](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dewey/)

The engine’s outcomes are categorical declarations: unmeasured, confirmed or disconfirmed. It asks for warranted revision rather than silently choosing a winning hypothesis. Experiments outside the software would require their own measurement and causal design.

## Argumentation, belief revision and automated reasoning

Dung formalizes the acceptability of arguments through relations among arguments. That literature motivates inspectable attacks and dependencies, but this implementation does not compute grounded, preferred or stable extensions. Its propositions, strict rules, defeasible links and objections form a deliberately smaller language. [Dung1995](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(94)00041-X)

The AGM tradition distinguishes belief contraction and revision and supplies constraints on rational change. This Lab preserves before/after commitments and rationales without claiming to satisfy AGM postulates. It permits inconsistent records without explosion, rather than treating every participant as a deductively closed classical theory. [AGM1985](https://doi.org/10.2307/2274239), [SEPBeliefRevision](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-belief-revision/)

A formal reasoner reasons over its representation. Authored strict implications are executable assumptions, not automatically certified translations of ordinary language. The browser and Python engines calculate the same bounded positive closure and grounded support. Negative commitments do not license arbitrary new positive conclusions. This formal scope is narrower than unrestricted theorem proving and much narrower than philosophical understanding. [SEPLogicAI](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-ai/)

## Reasoning quality and measurement validity

Facione’s consensus report and Kuhn’s work supply useful antecedents for analysis, evaluation and argument. They do not license treating this new eight-dimension rubric as an established test. King and Kitchener’s reflective judgment work highlights the role of justification under uncertainty; retention, qualification and suspension can all be appropriate. [Facione1990](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED315423), [Kuhn1991](https://doi.org/10.1017/cbo9780511571350), [KingKitchener1994](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED368925)

Kane’s argument-based validation framework requires warrant for the interpretations and uses of scores. A response-level judgment cannot be promoted directly into a stable person-level philosophical capacity. Missing evidence must remain missing; failure to answer does not establish inability or unwillingness. The study must examine response processes, rater calibration, dimensional structure, differential performance, transfer and consequences of interpretation. [Kane2013](https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.12000)

Agreement is also distinct from validity. Two raters can agree on a poor criterion. Quadratically weighted kappa is implemented for paired ordinal ratings; its degeneracy when there is no marginal variation is reported as not estimable. ICC selection requires an explicit model of raters, targets and intended generalization, so the Lab does not silently substitute one ICC variant for another. [Cohen1968](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0026256), [KooLi2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcm.2016.02.012)

The question bank has a structural balance audit, not independent evidence of fairness. Logic items can advantage consistency checking; ethics items may invite normative assumptions; practical consequences can be harder to operationalize in metaphysics. Domain balance does not remove those interactions. The assessment forms are distinct but not equated. Familiarity with the existing Depth Map must be measured because some authored questions share origins with it. Transfer cannot be inferred from immediate practice alone. [BarnettCeci2002](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.128.4.612), [Abrami2015](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654314551063)

## AI evaluation and experimental design

HELM motivates documenting more than a single success number: model identity, scenario, prompts, resource use and failures matter. The Lab fixes model settings across its four AI conditions and records generated questions rather than silently substituting a deterministic answer after an error. [Liang2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110)

Research on LLM judges identifies biases that make self-assessment unsuitable as independent evidence of effectiveness. Human outcome scoring is therefore blind to the exported assignment fields; method fidelity is reviewed separately with condition information. Text may nevertheless reveal its treatment, so masking is an attempted safeguard to measure rather than a guaranteed psychological state. [Zheng2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685)

Randomization, participant flow, exclusions and transparent reporting are central design obligations. The historical CONSORT 2010 statement is used as a reporting antecedent, not a claim that this software is a clinical trial or meets current clinical reporting requirements. Allocation in a public static client is reproducible but inspectable. A real study needs server-authoritative assignment, consent and authenticated collection before recruitment. [Schulz2010](https://www.bmj.com/content/340/bmj.c332.full.print)

## The defensible contribution

The present contribution is an executable comparison framework: a shared representation, four documented procedures, matched browser/Python traces, separate independent outcome and fidelity workflows, disjoint assessment pools and auditable records. It does not establish that any method improves reasoning, that these procedures exhaust their traditions, or that a single hierarchy of philosophical depth exists. Those are questions for conceptual criticism and properly approved empirical investigation.
