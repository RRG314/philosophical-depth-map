# Running Philosophical Depth Map

## The five parts of the site

- **Home:** a starting point, The Last Prisoner, an ocean preview, and routes into questions and reading.
- **Thought experiments:** scenarios and staged questions. A completed response can be saved privately on the reader's device, voluntarily submitted to the owner when collection is open, or discussed publicly through a separate post.
- **Learn:** brief introductions, examples, exercises, the reading directory, and ways to examine an argument. The existing Reasoning Lab and concept library remain available here.
- **Discuss:** topic conversations, experiment discussions and community proposals. Contributions enter moderation before publication.
- **My journey:** locally saved experiment reflections, existing question journal/bookmarks, ocean progress, and links to the concept map and community account activity.

The site keeps the name Philosophical Depth Map and credits original work to Steven Reid. A public contact address has not been supplied. No address or professional affiliation has been invented.

## Publishing an experiment

Once your account has its administrator role, open Editorial desk from the community navigation. Choose Experiments, start a new entry, and enter the scenario and initial question. Add a later condition only if the reader should see it after responding. Put background discussion in the context field so it follows the response sequence.

Use **Preview reader sequence** to check the order. Save as Draft while working. Set Publication to Published when ready. Featured and Display order control the collection. The homepage currently spotlights The Last Prisoner specifically; changing the home spotlight is a separate editorial change. Published edits create new versions, preserving the wording associated with already-submitted answers.

Review proposals before preparing one as a featured experiment. Credit its contributor and check the wording before publication. Moderate posts and reports from the other desk sections. Private answers are accessible to administrators, not ordinary moderators. Review/export controls explicitly describe the visible page of records.

## Local reflections and ocean progress

Save to My journey stores the exact scenario version and response in the existing device journal, never in the public forum. Saving the same experiment/version updates its local copy. Different versions may coexist, but each distinct experiment contributes only one exploration milestone. This is participation progress, not answer scoring. Private submissions and public posts are separate and are not deleted by removing a local reflection.

Export progress includes local reflections. Deleting all local data removes them. Older journal exports remain importable. Downloaded files should be kept private; the site cannot recover a journal after browser storage is cleared unless the reader has an export.
