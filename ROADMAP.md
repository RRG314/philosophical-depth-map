# Roadmap and gates

Costs are rough planning orders, not quotes. Hosting/model prices must be verified before purchase. Research labor and recruitment usually dominate static hosting. Dates depend on staffing and approvals.

| Version | Milestones / acceptance | Dependencies and research prerequisites | Complexity / planning cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Playable static release: eight domains, 160 draft items, fixed 16-item path, branch loop, graph, journal, transparent self-rubrics, achievements, export/import/delete, responsive ocean UI; passing build and browser checks; Pages deployment | No research enrollment; editor-reviewed structure is not calibration. Optional AI can remain unconfigured, with adapter tested | Moderate engineering; static hosting may fit free allowance. AI disabled by default; bounded usage only after price review |
| 2 | Authenticated accounts/sync, verified ownership and conflict resolution, consent/withdrawal service, rater/admin dashboard, full AI dialogue, approved exports; security and deletion tests | Identity/hosting credentials, processor agreements, ethics determination before recruitment, blinded rating workflow | High; weeks/months engineering plus operations; provision an explicit hosting and token budget |
| 3 | Run reliability, dimensionality, calibration, DIF/invariance and longitudinal analyses; publish uncertainty and failed models; no norms without defensible sampling | Frozen versions, registered analyses, simulation-based sample plan, independent human raters and diverse samples | High research cost; months; specialist/rater labor and recruitment budget established per study |
| 4 | Approved multi-site cohorts, preregistered trials and transfer studies, multilingual/cross-cultural review, peer-reviewed outputs and consent-compatible data releases | Institutional partners, translation validation, retention/security arrangements, replication resources | Very high; multi-institution support; funded study budgets rather than an app subscription estimate |
| 5 | Moderated debate, community argument maps, historical journeys, simulations, classroom mode, reviewed submissions and richer ocean environments | Moderation staffing, reporting/appeals, educator usability, new privacy/child-safety review where relevant | High product/operations; sustained human moderation and specialist review |

## Measurable gates

V1 software gate is operational, not psychometric: content counts, deterministic arithmetic, actual route/assessment/export tests and verified hosting. Scientific statements stay provisional. V2 cannot collect because a schema exists; require actual consent and withdrawal endpoints and authorization. V3 must report model failures and subgroup limitations, not just a successful optimizer. V4 preregistration precedes outcomes. V5 moderation must be working before public submissions open.

Current gaps and blocked external dependencies are tracked in CHECKLIST.md and VERIFICATION.md. No future feature is advertised as live.
